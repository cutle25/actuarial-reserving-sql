DATA DICTIONARY - synthetic personal auto book (not real data)
Evaluation date: 2025-06-30. Each policy is a 12-month term beginning at start_date.

policies.csv (one row per policy)
  policy_id        Unique policy identifier.
  state            State where the vehicle is garaged.
  vehicle_type     Sedan, SUV, Truck, Sports.
  driver_age       Age of the primary driver at policy start.
  start_date       First day of coverage.
  exposure         Earned exposure in policy-years as of the evaluation date:
                   the fraction of the 12-month term that has elapsed, capped at 1.
  annual_premium   Full-term premium for the 12 months, in dollars.

claims.csv (one row per claim)
  claim_id         Unique claim identifier.
  policy_id        The policy the claim was made under.
  loss_date        Date the loss occurred.
  paid_amount      Dollars paid on the claim to date.

payments.csv (one row per payment transaction)
  payment_id       Unique payment identifier.
  claim_id         The claim the payment was made on.
  payment_date     Date of the payment, YYYY-MM-DD.
  amount           Dollars paid in this transaction. Negative = money returned
                   (a recovery such as salvage or subrogation).
