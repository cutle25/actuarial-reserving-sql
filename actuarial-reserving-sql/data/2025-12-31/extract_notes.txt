EXTRACT NOTES - synthetic personal auto book (not real data)
Second extract of the same book. Evaluation date: 2025-12-31.
Prepared by the claims data team. Previous extract: evaluation date 2025-06-30.

Files
  policies.csv        one row per policy (full re-extract)
  claims.csv          one row per claim (full re-extract, includes claims reported since June)
  payments.csv        one row per payment transaction (full re-extract, all payments to 2025-12-31)
  control_totals.csv  totals from the claims system's month-end report for 2025-12-31.
                      Produced by the source system, independently of these extract files.

Columns are the same as the 2025-06-30 extract (see data/2025-06-30/data_dictionary.txt), except:
  policies.exposure   earned exposure as of 2025-12-31. Every policy term has now ended
                      (no new policies were written after 2024-12-31), so exposure is 1.0 for all.

Formats
  payments.payment_date   YYYY-MM-DD
  claims.loss_date        M/D/YYYY, as before

control_totals.csv
  table_name     policies, claims or payments
  period         'all', or the payment year for payments
  n_records      policies: number of unique policies; claims: number of claims;
                 payments: number of payment transactions dated in that year
  total_amount   claims: sum of paid_amount; payments: sum of amount; blank for policies

Loaded into SQL_workbook.db by sql/01_load_2025-12-31.sql as tables
policies_dec25, claims_dec25, payments_dec25, control_totals_dec25.
The 2025-06-30 tables (policies, claims, payments) are not touched.
